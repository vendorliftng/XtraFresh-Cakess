# XtraFresh Cakes — Site Copy (Lagos English)

Every string on the site, rewritten so a Lagos customer reads it once and understands it. Left column is what's live now; right column is the replacement.

---

## The rule we're following

**Plain Nigerian English — not heavy pidgin.**

You're selling ₦60,000–₦250,000 cakes. Full pidgin in body copy would undercut that; people read it as *cheap*. But the current copy has the opposite problem — "bespoke", "meticulously baked", "affordable luxury", "Interactive Studio" is Pinterest language, not Lagos language. It sounds imported, and imported reads as *this is not for me*.

The target is how a confident Lagos business actually talks: **direct, warm, no showing off.** A little local flavour in the loud places (banner, buttons), clean plain English everywhere money and logistics are discussed.

**"Tier" → "layer" everywhere.** Nobody in Lagos orders a "three-tier cake"; they order a **3-layer cake**. This alone removes a real point of confusion.

---

## Words to drop, and what to use instead

| Drop | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tier | **Layer** | What people actually say |
| Bespoke | Made-to-order, custom | "Bespoke" is a UK tailoring word |
| Affordable Luxury | Premium cakes, honest prices | Says nothing; every brand claims it |
| Meticulously baked | Baked fresh, never frozen | Concrete beats flowery |
| Interactive Studio | Build Your Cake | Say what it does |
| Chef's Specials | What's Hot Right Now | Warmer, and true |
| Finalize Order | Confirm Your Order | "Finalize" is stiff |
| Configurator | Cake Builder | Nobody says configurator |
| Full Catalog | See All Cakes | Plainer |
| Customer Favorites | Most Ordered | Factual, more persuasive |
| Vibrant flavors taking over our kitchen | The cakes everyone's ordering this month | Human |
| Serving All of Lagos State | Where We Deliver | Direct |
| Freshly Handcrafted in Lagos Daily | Baked fresh in Lagos every morning | Specific |

**Spelling:** British/Nigerian throughout — *flavour, colour, personalised, neighbourhood*. The site currently mixes American and British. Pick one.

**Money:** always `₦60,000` — with the symbol, with commas. Never "60k" in body copy, never "NGN".

## Prices

Every price shows as a range with an anchor:

> **₦55,000 – ₦72,000**
> Depends on design and finish. Most 8-inch orders come to about ₦60,000.
> *Final price confirmed on WhatsApp.*

That last line is not optional. A range with no explanation reads as *we'll decide when we see you*, which is exactly the fear you're trying to remove.

## Two things the copy is missing

**1. No trust signals anywhere.** A brand-new site asking for ₦60,000 and a delivery address, with no reviews, no baker's face, no history. Add before launch:

> **Why XtraFresh?** Baked fresh — never frozen, never day-old · Real ingredients, no shortcuts · You talk to the baker directly, not a call centre · Free delivery in Ogba, Ikeja & Ojodu

Add a short "who we are" line with a photo of the kitchen or the baker. On the Lagos mainland, knowing there's a real person and a real address behind the site converts better than any design work.

**2. Nothing sets expectations on timing.** Lead times appear nowhere except buried in the promo. People will order for tomorrow's party and be disappointed. Put **"Order at least 1 week ahead"** in the header, the builder, the checkout, and the footer.
